Daily briefs

The most widely covered Sri Lankan stories of a day, grouped across Sinhala, Tamil and English outlets and summarised by a small local model. Every sentence links to the articles it is based on. Machine-written: when every sentence was checked against its sources, 12 of 73 were wrong, often in names (audit). Check the sources before relying on a sentence.

Sinhala and Tamil briefs are machine translations of the English brief, sentence by sentence, made with NLLB-200 (distilled 600M), a dedicated translation model. The English brief itself is written by a 4-billion-parameter model (qwen3 4B) from the source articles. Expect awkward phrasing and occasional mistranslated names. Each translated sentence keeps the citations of the English sentence it came from.

Data: nuuuwan/lk_news by Nuwan Senaratna (MIT). Headlines and links belong to the original publishers.
